# CR Mariposa inquiry-data policy

Effective date: 2026-09-25. This operational policy supports the public bilingual privacy notice and should be reviewed with the business owner and Costa Rican counsel. It is not a substitute for legal advice.

## Purpose and access

- Store inquiry data only to answer a guest, recommend a home, and manage a possible or confirmed stay.
- Do not sell inquiry data or reuse it for unrelated marketing without separate consent.
- Administrators and client editors may read and update inquiries. Only administrators may delete them permanently.
- Use the minimum information needed. Do not copy sensitive personal data into inquiry notes.

## Consent evidence

Every new public-form inquiry records the accepted privacy-notice version, consent timestamp, and retention review date. A submission without explicit consent is rejected. WhatsApp, telephone, and email conversations remain governed by the notice supplied through those channels.

## Retention and deletion

The default review date is 24 months after submission. Quarterly, an administrator filters inquiries whose `retentionUntil` date has passed and deletes them unless:

- an active or recently completed stay requires the record;
- the guest asks for continued contact; or
- a documented legal or accounting obligation requires continued retention.

When an exception ends, delete the inquiry at the next quarterly review. A valid access, correction, withdrawal, or deletion request is handled promptly and documented without retaining unnecessary identity evidence.

## Incident handling

Limit access immediately, preserve relevant logs, assess affected information, inform the owner, and obtain legal advice about any required notice. Rotate compromised credentials and document the corrective action.
